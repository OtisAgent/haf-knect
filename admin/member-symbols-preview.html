Member symbols

A Plus mark for Plus members, a crown for Pro members — on every account type. Preview only, nothing is live.

Beside a name — how it looks in a list

On a dark screen

Your own account — top bar, signed in

On a profile header — with the word, where there is room

At the sizes it actually gets used

Who wears nothing

Free and Lite accounts carry no mark at all — that is what makes the two marks mean something. An account whose tier we have not been told also shows nothing, rather than guessing.

One gate decides who wears what, so a Lite account can never grow a mark and a Plus member can never grow a crown. The crown is drawn once and reused — there is only one crown in the business.

HAF KNECT

Work gets posted. Checked drivers take it.

HAF KNECT is the network where a job is posted once and the drivers who are cleared to do it can see it. This is the Demo Centre: it walks the product from three seats — the person posting the work, the driver doing it, and somebody getting cleared to drive — and ends with what each account level allows. Every screen named on this page is read out of the live product each time the page is built. It is not the live network, and nothing on it is real work.

How it works

Three moves, start to finish.

01

You post the work

Where it is collected, where it goes, what it is and when it has to be there. One screen. Any HAF account can post from the day it is opened.

02

The network sees it

It goes to the drivers who are cleared and near enough to do it. A job that cannot be placed comes off the board rather than sitting there going stale.

03

A checked driver takes it

Collection and delivery are each proved by the driver, and the job carries one reference from the moment it is posted to the moment it is paid.

What a job costs

One matrix, and it is the same one the network quotes with.

A price is not a haggle and it is not a guess. Four things set it: the vehicle, how fast the job has to happen, how far it goes, and how hard it is to load. Everything else on this page is a consequence of those four.

What else moves it

Named, capped and on the quote before anyone agrees to anything. No surprise on the invoice.

Short local work is charged less than the smallest charge would suggest: under miles a job comes down by up to %, easing back to the full minimum by miles. Fuel is the one thing that can move a rate after the fact, it only moves when pump prices run more than % over the market average, and it can never add more than %.

Joining

Three steps, and the first one is free.

01

Open an account

Your name, email, mobile and date of birth, your company if you have one, and a PIN. No payment and no documents to find.

02

Confirm your email

One click in the email we send. That is what opens the account and lets you post work.

03

Send documents if you want to drive

Only if you intend to take jobs. Clever checks them and releases you, and the driving side opens.

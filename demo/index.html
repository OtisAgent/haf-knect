HAF KNECT

Work gets posted. Checked drivers take it.

HAF KNECT is the network where a job is posted once and the drivers who are cleared to do it can see it. This is the Demo Centre: it walks the product from three seats — the person posting the work, the driver doing it, and somebody getting cleared to drive — and ends with what each account level allows. Every screen named on this page is read out of the live product each time the page is built. It is not the live network, and nothing on it is real work.

How it works

Three moves, start to finish.

01

You post the work

Where it is collected, where it goes, what it is and when it has to be there. One screen. Any HAF account can post from the day it is opened.

02

The network sees it

It goes to the drivers who are cleared and near enough to do it. A job that cannot be placed comes off the board rather than sitting there going stale.

03

A checked driver takes it

Collection and delivery are each proved by the driver, and the job carries one reference from the moment it is posted to the moment it is paid.

Getting checked
Clever, as a new driver sees it

How somebody gets cleared to drive.

Clever is the check, not the shop. Nobody pays it and nobody joins through it: a person opens their HAF account first, then Clever decides whether they are let near other people's work. These are its four stages, in order.

  1. Account
    They open a HAF account first, at join.usehaf.co.uk. Name, email, mobile, date of birth, company if there is one, and a PIN. No payment and no documents at this point.
  2. Documents
    They sign in to Clever and send what proves who they are and that the van is legal.
  3. Review
    The HAF team checks the documents against the person and the vehicle. This is the part that protects everybody already on the network.
  4. Access
    Clever releases them by name, and only then does the driving side open. Until that moment the account still works perfectly well for posting work.

The part people get wrong

There are two doors, and money only opens one of them.

Joining HAF and being cleared to drive for HAF are separate things. Keeping them separate is what makes the network worth being in.

Upgrade

Start with one HAF KNECT account, then add what you need when you are ready.

What would you like to add?

You do not need to decide this when you join. Choose a route here whenever it becomes useful. Your HAF login stays the same.

1

Owner driver

Confirm your business, then add your identity, driving and vehicle documents. Each completed step unlocks more of HAF PLNA and the network.

2

Fleet or delivery company

Confirm the company once, then add team members, drivers and vehicles as the operation grows.

3

I drive for a fleet

Your fleet adds you to its team. You use your own HAF login to complete the personal checks the fleet needs.

Nothing changes until you choose a route. You can keep posting and using your HAF KNECT account on Free.

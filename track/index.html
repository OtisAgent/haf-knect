Location sharing — terms & privacy

What we use: your device’s live location while this screen is open and sharing is on — only while you are on an active HAF job, never in your own time.

Live sharing: your current position is shown to the customer/business for this job so they can follow it. You appear by your driver name — your phone number is never shown.

We don’t keep a movement history: your live position is used only for this delivery and is cleared once it’s complete — we don’t build a trail of where you’ve been. To plan routes and improve coverage we use the job’s collection and delivery postcodes, not your personal location history.

Your control & rights: sharing is voluntary — tap Stop or close this page at any time to end it; nothing is shared while it is off. You can ask what we hold about you, or have it erased, at any time.

Data controller: HAF Logistics Ltd (useHAF). Lawful basis: carrying out your delivery work and HAF’s legitimate interest in running the network. Questions, access or erasure: privacy@usehaf.co.uk. By tapping Start you confirm you have read and agree to these terms.
